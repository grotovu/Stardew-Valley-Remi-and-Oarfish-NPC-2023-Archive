# [ARCHIVE] Stardew Valley Custom NPC Project: Remi & Oarfish (2023)

> **ARCHIVAL NOTICE:** This repository is a preserved historical archive of a personal custom NPC project developed in 2023. It is hosted here untouched for portfolio, data preservation, and demonstration purposes only. It is **unmaintained**, not updated for modern Stardew Valley versions, and provided strictly as-is.

---

### Project Contents
* **Original 2023 Content Patcher JSONs:** Over 1,000 lines of manual, hand-typed dialogue, seasonal schedules, custom events, and third-party mod integrations (Restauranteer, NPC Adventures).
* **Hand-Pixelled Assets:** custom portraits, complete overworld 4-directional walk cycles, fishing animations, and custom state sprites.
* **Original Lore:** The complete Stardew Valley adaptation of original characters Remi (Cluster Residue) and Oarfish Pearl.
